This Week in Skincare: What's Actually Worth the Hype

This week, the skincare scene is abuzz with two products: the TikTok-viral Danessa Myricks Yummy Skin Blurring Balm Powder and the new Glow Recipe Watermelon Milk Peptide Cushion Cream. We've stripped back the aesthetic packaging and influencer hype to see if these formulations actually deliver on their promises.

Trending #1

Danessa Myricks
Yummy Skin Blurring Balm Powder

Why Everyone's Talking About It

A viral sensation on TikTok for its "real-life filter" effect, this product gained massive traction after being featured in "Get Ready With Me" videos referencing the high-glam, blurred aesthetics of shows like EUPHORIA. It promises to control oil while maintaining a skin-like finish.

What's Actually In It

Magnesium CarbonateOil absorption
IsohexadecaneTexture enhancer
DimethiconeSmoothing silicone
SqualaneLightweight moisture
Tocopheryl AcetateVitamin E / Antioxidant

Dermatologist's Take

"The balm-to-powder technology is impressive for texture management. It uses Upsalite (magnesium carbonate) which absorbs sweat and sebum more effectively than traditional talc. However, it requires a silicone-based primer for the smoothest application on textured skin."

Who Should Try

Normal, Oily, & Combination skin.

Skip If

You have very dry skin or active flaking.

Viral Alert

Glow Recipe
Watermelon Milk Peptide Cushion Cream

Why Everyone's Talking About It

Riding the wave of "peptide-forward" skincare, this cushion cream targets the "skin barrier repair" trend. It's positioned as a lightweight yet deeply nourishing alternative to heavy winter creams, appealing to those seeking the signature Glow Recipe radiance without the stickiness.

What's Actually In It

PeptidesFirming & Repair
GlycerinHumectant moisture
SqualaneBarrier mimicry
PanthenolSoothing Vitamin B5
CeramidesLipid replenishment

Dermatologist's Take

"The mix of panthenol and peptides provides genuine long-lasting hydration. It's particularly well-suited for those with a compromised barrier from over-exfoliation. It lacks heavy occlusives, making it safer for acne-prone skin that still needs moisture."

Who Should Try

Sensitive & All skin types needing hydration.

Skip If

You have no major barrier or hydration concerns.

Bottom Line

While both products are aesthetically pleasing and highly rated by the community, they serve very different purposes. Danessa Myricks is a specialized tool for texture and oil control—perfect for long days or events. Glow Recipe is a soothing daily staple for barrier health.

"Our recommendation? If you're oily and want that filtered look, go with Danessa. If your skin is feeling tight or irritated, Glow Recipe is your winner."

This bulletin is for educational purposes only. Consult a dermatologist for personalised advice.
